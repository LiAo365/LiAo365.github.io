abstract: >-
  For real-world high-resolution pavement images where the distress foreground is scarce and ordinary downsampling discards over 95% of pixels, we use interpretability analysis to guide the network design and propose the plug-and-play Adaptive Perception Module (APM): large-kernel convolution with residual downsampling preserves rich low-level features while an attention mechanism activates distress regions and suppresses background; trained by joint learning at no extra supervision cost, improving classification accuracy to 84.47%.

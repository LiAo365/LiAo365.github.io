abstract: >-
  Pavement distress occupies a tiny fraction of high-res road images, so standard downsampling loses most of the signal. We let interpretability analysis guide the architecture and propose the Adaptive Perception Module (APM) — large-kernel convolutions with residual downsampling to keep low-level detail, plus attention to highlight damage regions. Plug-and-play, no extra supervision; classification accuracy up to 84.47%.
